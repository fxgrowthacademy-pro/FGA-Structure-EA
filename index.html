FAQ

Everything important, without the clutter.

Is FGA Structure EA for sale?

No. It is offered as an account-authorized access benefit for eligible FGA partner clients.

Does Exness registration guarantee EA access?

No. The submitted MT5 account must first be verified as eligible under the FGA partner-access program.

Can every existing Exness user change their IB/partner?

No such guarantee is made. Contact official Exness support and follow the eligibility/process they provide.

What does “up to 60% trade rebate” mean?

It refers to a maximum share of eligible partner-generated spread/commission compensation returned under qualifying FGA rebate terms. It does not mean 60% of losses, deposits or all trading costs.

Does FGA Structure EA guarantee profit?

No. Automated trading involves substantial risk and results can change with market conditions, execution, settings and account risk.

Is a VPS required?

No. A suitable computer can run MT5. A VPS is an optional convenience for remote uptime.
