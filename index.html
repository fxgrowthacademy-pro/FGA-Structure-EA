FAQ

Clear answers. No clutter.

Is FGA Structure EA for sale?

No. It is offered as an account-authorized access benefit for eligible FGA partner clients.

Does IC Markets registration guarantee EA access?

No. The submitted MT5 account must first be verified as eligible under the FGA partner-access program.

Can every existing IC Markets client change their IB/partner?

No guarantee is made. Existing clients can request a partner change by emailing partners@ic.com. After IC Markets sends confirmation of the change, the client can send the MT5 Account ID to FX Growth Academy on Telegram for verification. IC Markets controls eligibility, approval and processing.

What is the Free Secret Strategy eBook?

It is a complimentary educational PDF for traders interested in manual trading. It focuses on a structured way to think about potential entries, holding decisions and exits. It is educational material and does not guarantee trading results.

Does FGA Structure EA guarantee profit?

No. Automated trading involves substantial risk and results can change with market conditions, execution, settings and account risk.

Is a VPS required?

No. A suitable computer can run MT5. A VPS is an optional convenience for remote uptime.
